# AVUQO product, category, and company-area previews
Date: 30 October 2026
Product owner direction: inspect full individual product pages and compare category-led versus company-led layouts for Cadbury, Craft Buddy, Petlibro, AKKO and MonsGeek. Longer term, companies may wish to sponsor their own destination areas.

## Structure chosen
1. **Independent product is primary**: /products/[slug] remains the public, verified-source-only product URL. A distinct product's listing, prices and rankings are not owned by a brand or advertiser.
2. **Category experience frames the decision**: chocolate/confectionery, crafts/hobbies, keyboards, and pet care receive specific editorial framing, comparisons and checklists. This is the default consumer path.
3. **Brand directory is optional**: a brand-filtered editorial area can aggregate eligible items. Brand names can identify records, but no official logos, endorsed claims or media are assumed.
4. **Paid company takeovers are separate**: authorised branded content, prominently labelled sponsored panels and paid campaigns require contracts, creative rights, accessibility and advertising review. They never alter AVUQO Score, organic rankings, price sorting or evidence.
5. **Every staged preview remains private**: eight product drafts and five branded areas are rendered only after owner/admin authorisation using server-side read privileges. No consumer product route is changed and no unverified price/image is displayed.

## Layouts available now
- The eight gallery cards have individual deep links to full product layouts and category editorial variations.
- Every deep page includes hero, image placeholder, unique guidance, exact identity checklists, evidence review and links to its brand area.
- Each of five company areas previews a hero, browsing themes, draft product collection and separation between editorial, sponsored and comparative content.
- The private brand index is linked in Advertising & Growth. All preview routes have noindex directives.
- Staged data is the source of product names/IDs, but editorial wording is proposed until independently verified. Placeholder artwork is AVUQO generated markup, not borrowed merchant assets.

## Publication gates
Merchant source permissions and current prices, exact product matching/variants, photography rights, brand use and claims, sponsored disclosure/contract if applicable, and accessible mobile/keyboard testing must be approved before public release. Draft identity does not mean permission to sell.

## Next authoring iteration
Owner-editable copy blocks, upload/review rights-cleared media, recorded approval history and category-specific comparison attributes, with preview/publish/rollback. No silent auto-publish.
