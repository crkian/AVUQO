# AVUQO category destinations: editorial design and launch policy
Date: 30 October 2026
Implementation PR: feature/category-destinations-distinct-20261030

## Goal
Each category must feel distinct: not a uniform template swapping a heading, but a recognisable experience with its own visual tone, introductory editorial stance, meaningful discovery clusters, buying guidance and searches. Reuse accessible shared infrastructure and AVUQO trust standards.

## This feature
- Ten existing lifestyle routes receive distinct themed editorial sections and search paths: Shopping, Travel, Home, Technology, Gifts, Toys, Beauty, Fashion, Motoring and Experiences.
- Existing page-specific hero photography and tips remain. Each additional editorial identity has unique copy, purpose, prompts, and accessible coloured discovery panels.
- Public /categories becomes a visually richer category atlas. The navigation's Categories dropdown becomes a grouped three-column explorer on desktop, and a readable one-column list on mobile.
- Other dedicated journeys, including Pets, Groceries, Christmas and Compare, retain their specialised pages and are linked from the atlas.
- Search suggestions are static editorial prompts that open the existing verified search. They do not imply inventory, autocomplete ranking, active merchant feeds or live stock.
- No database mutation, product publishing, AI spending or category activation.
- These curated entries cover currently implemented public category routes, not the ~1,376 inactive staged taxonomy categories. Keep inactive categories private until route, content, rights and eligible product verification.

## Next expansion gates
1. Test touch, pointer and keyboard focus in desktop mega menu, including owner changes to navigation order.
2. Visually inspect 375px/768px/1024px/1440px widths, 125%/150% browser zoom, and reduced-motion behaviour.
3. Introduce owner-controlled category editorial fields and approved imagery, with preview and rollback, before scaling to dozens or thousands of routes.
4. Connect verified product inventory and canonical identities only after source rights and quality checks.
5. Add category-aware search assistance only when suggestions can be grounded in authorised category and product data.
6. Keep outside Site Advertising and four-brand Social Marketing isolated from independent AVUQO category ranking.
