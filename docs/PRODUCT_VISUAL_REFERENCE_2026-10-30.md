# AVUQO private product storefront visual reference
Owner-provided screenshots: an online UK retailer's homepage banners, seasonal category tiles, multi-column navigation and search-assistance patterns. These are inspiration for standard shopping UX, not authorised assets.

## AVUQO pattern decisions
- Make search prominent with accurate category discovery.
- Use a visually clear, pausable editorial/campaign carousel.
- Feature department tiles under the banner, but no false discounts, delivery promises, click-and-collect, merchant imagery, stock or store claims.
- Keep AVUQO original styling and independent value assessment, not a replica of the retailer.
- Third-party AVUQO site advertising differs from marketing our four sites externally.
- All live product cards require explicit merchant feed and image rights, valid product identity, eligible offers and truthful source evidence.
