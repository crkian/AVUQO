**Implementation date:** 20 November 2026, morning GMT. **Branch:** `feature/shop-all-verified-offers-and-private-tyre-crossmatch-20261120`. **Release status:** CANDIDATE pending full CI, GitHub merge, exact Vercel production READY, production DB migration, service-role privilege verification, authenticated owner page and public HTTP evidence.

## Strategic objective
The owner wants an **online retail park**, not a small static offers shelf: all real shops, a useful catalogue, and *genuinely* cheap like-for-like products. AVUQO must remain independent, preserve every original page/25 categories/21 advertiser brand pages and the warm visual system, and treat source rights and data quality as hard gates. This release makes two complementary advances: a wide public catalogue of already approved retailer prices, and a **private** proof-based cross-retailer identity pipeline for future tyres. It does NOT claim every joined advertiser has a product feed, nor that public tyre prices across two merchants are ready.

## New public feature: `/shop`
- Search by title/model/brand wording (up to 80 safe characters), filter approved seller and available published feed-category facets, sort **item prices** low-to-high/high-to-low or newest, and browse deterministic **36 published candidate rows per page** up to a bounded page index.
- Facets are drawn dynamically from published candidate records but only for database merchant IDs mapped to owner-accepted GB Awin programmes. Internal sample/demo merchant entries are omitted. Facet counts are **published candidates, not claims of live availability**.
- Every candidate offer is rechecked through existing `verifiedOffers({offerIds})` on each request. It retains owner emergency and deal publishing switches, programme accepted evidence, approved first-publication, exact reviewed price, fresh deal score, 48h Awin source check, GBP, stock, match confidence, allowed source rights and own-publisher tracking. No private `feed_import_records` or unreviewed catalog is displayed.
- Product detail uses existing canonical active product slug and consumer-safe description/media. Deals are grouped by canonical product only within this page window; additional sellers for the exact item are confirmed on the full individual product detail. Item-price sorting never implies lowest **delivered total**, since Awin delivery evidence is currently intentionally null after the public admission gate.
- `/shop` linked from homepage catalogue gateway, `/catalogue`, `/products`, `/partners`, global `/search` (fixes false "no results" from only latest 60), safe navigation and sitemap. Existing 25 categories, `/marketplace`, individual 21 partner shop pages, shopping pages and other specialist routes are retained.

## Product comparison truthfulness
- Pure `compareEligibleOffers` helper counts **distinct merchants**, not offers. One independent seller: "Verified item price at this retailer", never "cheapest market". Multiple independently admitted sellers: sort by source-verified **item price** if any delivery total unknown; compare **complete totals** only if every offer provides evidenced total >= item price. Awin total_price intentionally hidden without dedicated delivery verification. Copy explicitly warns when cheapest delivered cost remains unknown.
- A customer may visit the wider `/shop` from a product detail or merchant-specific offer page, with a prefilled brand/merchant filter. This is shopping discovery, NOT cross-SKU fuzzy equivalence or a claim that different merchant stock is identical.
